# Privacy Policy — StepNotate

**Last updated:** November 2026

StepNotate is a browser extension for annotating PDF and image documents. This policy is short because the extension is built to keep everything on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. StepNotate has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no telemetry of any kind.

- **Documents and annotations** you create are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB) and are never uploaded anywhere.
- **Preferences** (theme, last used tool) are stored locally in your browser.
- **Signatures and stamps** you draw or import stay on your device.

## What we access

- Files you explicitly open — via the file picker, drag-and-drop, a link you right-click, or a URL you enter.
- The address of the current tab, only to offer opening it in StepNotate.

The extension never reads page content, never injects scripts into pages, and never transmits your data.

## Third parties

StepNotate makes no network requests except fetching the document URL you explicitly ask it to open. No third parties receive any information through this extension.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published in this repository.

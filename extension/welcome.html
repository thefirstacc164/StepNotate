01

Open

Click the StepNotate icon and pick a file, or drop a PDF, PNG or JPG straight into the workspace. Right-click any PDF link on the web and choose Open link in StepNotate.

02

Mark

Select text to highlight, underline or strike it. Pen, marker, shapes, text boxes, sticky notes, signatures and image stamps sit in one toolbar. Shortcuts are one key away.

03

Keep

Work saves itself to this browser as you go. Export a flattened PDF, a PNG, or a project file you can reopen and keep editing later. No account, no watermarks.

Keep it within reach

Click the puzzle-piece icon in your toolbar, then the pin beside StepNotate. It will stay next to your address bar.
